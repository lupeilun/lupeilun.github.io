C40B Drive Bridge

Personal Google Drive Integration

C40B Drive Bridge is a private, single-user utility that connects a personal computer to the owner’s Google Drive.

The application uses Google Drive to read task instructions authorized by the owner and to save the corresponding response files.

The application is intended solely for its owner’s personal use. It does not provide public accounts, commercial services, or access to other users’ Google Drive data.

Google Drive access

The application requests Google Drive access to:

* Read designated task documents.
* Create and read response files.
* Manage files required for its authorized operations.

Access is authorized through Google OAuth and can be revoked by the Google account owner.

Data handling

The application runs on the owner’s own computer. Google Drive is used to exchange task and response files. No independent public data-processing service is operated by this website.

This website is informational only. It does not provide a login form or collect personal information through its pages.
